  (unlimited fanin and depth=1 fanin) now share one predicate instead of two
  drift-prone copies.

  identifiers cannot contain those characters, so this reliably identifies a
  part-select slice node without touching source text (no new source-regex

  `{a[2:5]}`); multi-driver bus-copy parents deliberately keep old behavior.
  These are intended per the validation doc, not defects.
- Direction-aware mapping (`[0:7]` ascending vs `[7:0]` descending) is
  unchanged by this commit and verified against P198.8 expectations.
