## 1. Verification 1 — fixture really contains both aliases, and they are dangling

Built the new fixture with `UnifiedTracer` and inspected the `SignalGraph`
(networkx `DiGraph`) directly. Results:

| Alias | present | `node.parent` | `bit_range` | parent width | in_degree (preds) | out edges |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bin2gray.gray_out[31:0]` | yes | `bin2gray.gray_out` | `[31:0]` | `(31, 0)` | **0** | `BIT_SELECT` → parent |
| `bin2gray.bin_in[31:0]` | yes | `bin2gray.bin_in` | `[31:0]` | `(31, 0)` | **0** | `BIT_SELECT` → parent; `DRIVER` → expression |

Both aliases satisfy every predicate of `_is_dangling_bit_alias`:
`node.parent == signal_id`, `bit_range` equals the full range derived from
parent `width`, the connecting edge is `BIT_SELECT`, and `predecessors == []`.
A graph-wide scan for `[31:0]` nodes returned exactly these two — no others.

The **old** fixture (`assign gray_out = bin_in ^ (bin_in >> 1);`) produced **no**
`[31:0]` nodes at all (preds N/A). So the prior audit finding was correct and the
new fixture genuinely exercises the structural-alias path.
